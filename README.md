A complete, original cooking game for the make.fun bounty
[best-cooking-game-20261125-0137](https://make.fun/bounty/best-cooking-game-20261125-0137).
You are the cook at an Indonesian street food warung. Orders come in on the
order board (nasi goreng, mie goreng, sate ayam, es teh and more). Take
ingredients from the counter, chop them on the cutting board, cook them in
the wok with the right timing, assemble the plate and serve it before the
customer's patience runs out. Earn coins, build combos and survive 3 days
of rising rush. Fail 3 orders in a day and the warung closes.

All art is drawn in code on canvas and all sounds are synthesized with
WebAudio. No external files, no libraries, no network requests.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or serve the `game/` folder
with any static server (for example `node serve.ts`, then open
http://localhost:8080).

- WASD: move the cook
- Space / E: use the station in front of you
- Q: throw away what you are holding
- P: pause, M: mute, R: restart
